Candidate Profile

Who Can Apply?

We welcome educators who possess strong subject command and genuine teaching enthusiasm

Strong Subject Knowledge

In-depth understanding of basic concepts and ability to simplify complex topics for CBSE & HSCE students.

Excellent Communication Skills

Clear, effective verbal skills to interact easily with students, fellow faculty members, and parents.

Passion for Teaching & Mentoring

Dedicating attention to student academic growth, doubt clearance, and building strong foundational learning habits.

Teaching Experience

Prior coaching or teaching experience is an advantage. Energetic educators seeking growth are highly encouraged.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common queries about teaching opportunities at Adarsha Pathasala

Where is Adarsha Pathasala located?

Adarsha Pathasala is located in Beguniapada, Ganjam District, Odisha (Pin: 761031). It is easily accessible by local public & private transport.

Can freshers apply for teaching positions?

Yes! Passionate freshers with strong subject knowledge and enthusiasm for teaching students are welcome to apply.

Which classes & boards are taught?

We offer specialized coaching for CBSE and HSCE (Odisha State Board) students from Classes 6 to 10.

How do I submit my Resume / CV?

Upload your Resume/CV to Google Drive, OneDrive, or Dropbox, copy the shareable link ("Anyone with the link can view"), and paste it in the application form below.
